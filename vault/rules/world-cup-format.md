# FIFA World Cup Format

The FIFA World Cup consists of two main stages:

1. Group Stage
2. Knockout Stage (Slutspel)

## Group Stage

At the start of the tournament, the teams are divided into groups.

Example:

| Group A |
|----------|
| Team 1 |
| Team 2 |
| Team 3 |
| Team 4 |

Each team plays against every other team in its group once.

For a group with 4 teams, each team plays:

- 3 matches
- One match against each opponent

### Points System

Teams earn points based on their results:

| Result | Points |
|----------|----------|
| Win | 3 |
| Draw | 1 |
| Loss | 0 |

### Group Ranking

After all matches have been played, teams are ranked by:

1. Total points
2. Goal difference (Goals Scored - Goals Conceded)
3. Goals scored
4. Other FIFA tie-breakers if needed

Example final standings:

| Team | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Team A | 7 |
| Team B | 5 |
| Team C | 3 |
| Team D | 1 |

### Qualification for the Knockout Stage

The top two teams in each group advance to the knockout stage.

✅ 1st place advances  
✅ 2nd place advances  
❌ 3rd place is eliminated  
❌ 4th place is eliminated

## Knockout Stage (Slutspel)

The knockout stage is a single-elimination tournament.

If a team loses a match, it is eliminated.

### Round of 16

The group winners play runners-up from other groups.

Example:

- Winner Group A vs Runner-up Group B
- Winner Group B vs Runner-up Group A

### Quarter-finals

The winners from the Round of 16 advance to the Quarter-finals.

### Semi-finals

The winners from the Quarter-finals advance to the Semi-finals.

### Third-place Match

The two losing semi-finalists play a match to determine third place.

### Final

The winners of the Semi-finals play in the Final.

The winner of the Final becomes:

🏆 FIFA World Cup Champion

## What Happens if a Knockout Match Ends in a Draw?

Unlike the group stage, knockout matches cannot end in a draw.

If the score is tied after 90 minutes:

1. Extra Time is played (2 x 15 minutes).
2. If still tied, a Penalty Shootout decides the winner.

## Tournament Flow

```text
Group Stage
     ↓
Round of 16
     ↓
Quarter-finals
     ↓
Semi-finals
     ↓
Final
     ↓
World Cup Champion
```

## Summary

- Teams are divided into groups.
- Every team plays every other team in its group.
- Wins = 3 points, Draws = 1 point.
- The top two teams from each group advance.
- The knockout stage is single elimination.
- Draws in knockout matches are decided by extra time and penalties.
- The winner of the final becomes World Cup Champion.
